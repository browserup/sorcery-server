Benefits

Reduce mean time to resolution

Click stack traces in Datadog, Sentry, or New Relic and open the exact line instantly. No searching, no copy-paste.

Editor-agnostic team sharing

Share one link that opens in each teammate's preferred editor. VS Code, IntelliJ, Neovim—everyone gets their own.

Eliminate context switching

Build errors, test failures, and code review comments become direct links to your editor. Stay in flow.

Open source, open standard

MIT licensed. The srcuri:// protocol is documented and extensible. No vendor lock-in.
